Street Duel: The Rise of Fighters
A fast-paced 3D arcade fighting game built with Unity, designed for quick, skill-based combat and an engaging player experience.
________________________________________
🚀 Overview
Street Duel: The Rise of Fighters is a 3D fighting game developed as a Final Year Design Project (FYDP). The game focuses on delivering short, intense battles with simple controls, making it accessible to casual players while still offering depth through combos and strategy.
Players fight against AI opponents in dynamic arenas, using a combination of attacks, defense, and timing to win matches and progress.
________________________________________
✨ Key Features
•	⚔️ 1v1 Fighting System
Real-time combat with responsive controls for movement, attacks, and defense.
•	🤖 AI Opponents (Multiple Difficulties)
Fight against intelligent AI with increasing challenge levels.
•	🔥 Combo & Combat Mechanics
Execute attack combinations for enhanced damage and engaging gameplay.
•	❤️ Health System
Dynamic health tracking with win/lose conditions.
•	🏟️ Immersive Arenas
Fight in visually styled environments that enhance the gameplay experience.
•	🎯 Simple & Intuitive Controls
Designed for ease of use using on-screen buttons and joystick.
